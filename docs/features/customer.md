# Feature: Customer Experience & Service Discovery

## 1. Capabilities

- **8 Standard Trade Categories:** Visual icons and service filters for Electrician, Plumber, AC Repair, Appliance Maintenance, Carpentry, Painting, Cleaning, and IT Support.
- **Custom Requirement Posting:** Multi-step wizard allowing customers to attach photos, specify visit schedules, and post open requirements for contractor bidding.
- **Instant Booking Confirmation:** Transparent checkout with address selection, emergency notes, and total fee breakdown (`BookingAddressNotesCard`, `BookingActionButton`).
- **Live Dispatch Radar:** Interactive radar animation displaying searching status and assigned specialist arrival progress.
