# Feature: Specialist & Worker Operations

## 1. Capabilities

- **Incoming Request Triage:** Filterable dispatch feed (`IncomingRequestsSegmentHeader`) showing distance, price, and customer address.
- **Availability Toggle:** Instant toggle between "Online (Ready for Dispatches)" and "Offline" (`WorkerAvailabilitySection`).
- **Rate & Service Zone Management:** Configure hourly labor rates and service travel radius (`WorkerRateZoneSelector`).
- **Active Job Screen:** Displays customer phone, arrival OTP entry keypad, active work stopwatch, and photo completion uploader (`ActiveJobActionButton`, `ActiveJobEscrowBanner`).
- **Trade Verification:** Upload NID and trade certificates to undergo review by SKAVIA operations before receiving dispatches.
